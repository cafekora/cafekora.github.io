Gambian Kora player Sura Susso is doing an interesting mini-tour in the UK, with several gigs in Wales and one in Cornwall. The tour also features Mari Mathias on guitar and vocals, Anghara Iris on flute, and Amadou Diagne on percussion. 

It's described as a musical fusion of Mandinka and Welsh cultures.

20th February: Galeri, Caernarfon

21st February: Aberystwyth Arts Centre, Aberystwyth

25th February: Acapela Studio, Cardiff

6th March: The Queens Hall, Narberth

11th April: Wanderfal Festival, The Cornish Bank, Falmouth

Details for tickets here: <https://chanyaculture.com/>
