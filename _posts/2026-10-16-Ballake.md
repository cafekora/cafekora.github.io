Ballaké Sissoko will release his new album, "Djelimady N°1," on November 27.

The album is dedicated to his father, and is produced by Lucy Durán, who was behind "New Ancient Strings," with engineer Jerry Boys, known for his work on the Buena Vista Social Club.

A small YouTube taster is here: <https://www.youtube.com/shorts/R6Y9MYItWzM>
