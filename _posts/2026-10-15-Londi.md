Gambian Kora player Sura Susso has a new album. LONDI (Knowledge), which is available on all streaming platforms! 🎧

He's promoting it as part of the Manding Melody UK tour, with his band, and Senegalian-born Malian guitarist, Habib Koité, and members of his band Bamada, who also feature on the album.

22 October – 1 November 2026

- Thursday, October 22, 2026: South Street Arts Centre, Reading.
- Friday, October 23, 2026: Bristol Beacon – Lantern Hall, Bristol.
- Saturday, October 24, 2026: Turner Sims, Southampton.
- Sunday, October 25, 2026: The Tung Auditorium (Liverpool).
- Tuesday, October 27, 2026: Band on the Wall, Manchester.
- Friday, October 30, 2026: Ropetackle Arts Centre, Shoreham.
- Saturday, October 31, 2026: The Old Malthouse (Chidham Village Hall), Chidham.

Details for tickets here: <https://www.surasusso.co.uk/>
